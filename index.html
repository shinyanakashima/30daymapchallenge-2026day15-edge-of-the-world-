    About this map

    What is wrong with this map

    This is a stereographic projection. It keeps angles correct, so small shapes look right, but sizes grow the farther you go from the centre.

    On a Mercator map, things get bigger the farther they are from the equator. On this map, things get bigger the farther they are from the centre.

    The centre here is the antipode of the place you chose — the point on the exact opposite side of the Earth. The antipode of the centre would be stretched to infinite size, so the place you chose cannot be drawn as a point. It wraps around the whole map as its outer edge. The area within 500 km of it (the red band) fills 89% of the map, and the rest of the world is squeezed into the middle.

    Notes
